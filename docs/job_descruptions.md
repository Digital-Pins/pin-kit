1. PhiUSIIL - Manager (President)

    Definition: The President oversees the entire organization, ensuring strategic alignment across all departments. This role is responsible for making high-level decisions and guiding the overall direction of the company.
    Qualifications: Extensive experience in leadership, strategic planning, and business management. Familiarity with AI-driven solutions is a plus.
    Responsibilities:
        Provide strategic direction and leadership across the organization.
        Manage relationships with directors and ensure alignment with company goals.
        Oversee the integration of all departments to ensure smooth operations.
    Relationships:
        Direct Reporting:
            Marketing Director (Wholesale Customers)
            HR Director (Security & HR)
            Finance Director (PhiSNAIL)
        Feedback Loop:
            Receives operational feedback from PhiSNAIL (Finance Director) regarding budget allocations and financial health.
            Provides strategic directions back to all directors based on insights from the AI-Powered Job Market Insights and ARCD support feedback.

2. PhiSNAIL - Finance Director

    Definition: The Finance Director is responsible for managing the organization's financial health, budgeting, and providing financial insights that guide strategic decisions.
    Qualifications: Experience in financial management, budgeting, and analysis. Familiarity with financial software and AI tools.
    Responsibilities:
        Manage financial planning, analysis, and budgeting.
        Ensure all departments are operating within budgetary constraints.
        Provide financial reports to the President and relevant departments.
    Relationships:
        Direct Reporting: Reports to the President (PhiUSIIL).
        Feedback Loop:
            Collaborates with the AI-Powered Job Market Insights for predictive financial modeling.
            Provides financial insights to the Supermarket department for sales analysis and inventory management.

3. Wholesale Customers - Marketing Director

    Definition: The Marketing Director focuses on the marketing strategy and execution, particularly targeting wholesale customers. This role ensures that the company’s products and services reach the right audience.
    Qualifications: Strong background in marketing, especially in B2B (business-to-business) environments. Experience in digital marketing and customer segmentation.
    Responsibilities:
        Develop and implement marketing strategies targeting wholesale customers.
        Analyze market trends and adjust marketing tactics accordingly.
        Work with the Sales and Supermarket departments to align marketing with sales goals.
    Relationships:
        Direct Reporting: Reports to the President (PhiUSIIL).
        Feedback Loop:
            Receives customer insights from the Sales department.
            Provides market feedback and customer preferences back to Sales and Inventory Management for product development and stock management.

4. Security & HR - HR Director

    Definition: The HR Director is responsible for all HR-related activities, including hiring, employee management, and ensuring a secure working environment.
    Qualifications: Experience in human resources management, employee relations, and workplace safety.
    Responsibilities:
        Oversee recruitment, onboarding, and employee management.
        Ensure workplace security and compliance with safety regulations.
        Manage employee benefits and performance evaluations.
    Relationships:
        Direct Reporting: Reports to the President (PhiUSIIL).
        Feedback Loop:
            Collaborates with the Technical Support team for workplace security enhancements.
            Provides HR data and insights to the ARCD and AI-Powered Job Market Insights teams for workforce planning and predictive analytics.

5. Supermarket - Ordering, Invoicing, and Sales Analysis

    Definition: This department manages all supermarket operations, from ordering and invoicing to sales analysis.
    Qualifications: Experience in retail management, supply chain logistics, and sales analysis.
    Responsibilities:
        Manage day-to-day supermarket operations, including inventory and sales.
        Analyze sales data to optimize inventory levels and ordering.
        Work with the Marketing and Inventory Management departments to align stock with customer demand.
    Relationships:
        Direct Reporting: Reports to the Finance Director (PhiSNAIL).
        Feedback Loop:
            Provides sales and inventory data to the Finance Director for budgetary planning.
            Receives marketing strategies and customer insights from the Marketing Director.

6. AI-Powered Job Market Insights

    Definition: This function provides predictive insights into the job market, helping align company operations with market trends.
    Qualifications: Expertise in AI, data analytics, and market research.
    Responsibilities:
        Develop and maintain AI models that predict job market trends.
        Provide actionable insights to HR, Marketing, and Finance departments.
        Continuously refine models based on new data and market changes.
    Relationships:
        Direct Reporting: Reports to the President (PhiUSIIL).
        Feedback Loop:
            Collaborates with HR and Finance to provide insights that influence hiring and financial planning.
            Receives data from Sales, Marketing, and Inventory departments to improve predictive accuracy.

    Qualifications: Experience in customer service, technical support, and problem resolution.
    Responsibilities:
        Provide technical support for internal systems and customers.
        Manage customer inquiries and ensure timely resolution of issues.
        Collaborate with other departments to improve product and service quality.
    Relationships:
        Direct Reporting: Reports to the Supermarket department.
        Feedback Loop:
            Provides customer feedback to the Marketing and Inventory departments.

    Qualifications: Experience in sales management, inventory control, and supply chain logistics.
    Responsibilities:
        Oversee sales operations and manage customer relationships.

    Relationships:
        Direct Reporting: Reports to the Supermarket department.
        Feedback Loop:

    Relationships:
